About Untrained Momentum

Enterprise IT experience without enterprise nonsense.

Untrained Momentum was built around a simple idea: technology should remove friction, not create more of it.

Built for real-world problems.

After more than a decade working in enterprise IT, I saw the same pattern over and over: the tools usually were not the whole problem. The real problem was disconnected systems, unclear ownership, repetitive work, and people being expected to figure everything out on their own.

Small businesses deal with the same problems, just without an internal IT department, project team, or process analyst sitting nearby. That is where Untrained Momentum fits.

I work across websites, support, automation, documentation, onboarding, business systems, AI, and operations because those things rarely exist in isolation. The goal is not to sell another tool. The goal is to make the tools you already have work better together.

What you can expect

  • Plain-English communication
  • Practical solutions
  • No unnecessary software pile-on
  • Remote-first efficiency
  • Clear next steps
  • One point of contact
